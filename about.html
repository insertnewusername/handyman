About L&G Aust

L&G Aust Pty Ltd was founded on the principle that quality handyman services should be accessible, transparent, and reliable. We’ve grown from a one‑person operation to a trusted team of skilled tradespeople, all while maintaining the same commitment to excellence.

We understand that your home or business is your biggest asset. That’s why we treat every project with the care and attention it deserves. Whether it’s a quick fix or a major overhaul, we bring expertise, honesty, and a friendly attitude to every job.

  • Over 10 years of combined experience
  • Fully insured and licensed
  • Transparent pricing – no surprises
  • We clean up after ourselves – guaranteed
